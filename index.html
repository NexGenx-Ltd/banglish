Powerful Learning Architecture

Everything You Need for Complete Fluency

Designed with Duolingo's playful micro-learning pace and Notion's structured clarity. Four foundational pillars that build genuine bilingual mastery.

Vocabulary Builder

Expand your active vocabulary with 5,000+ categorized words. Uses intelligent Spaced Repetition (SRS) algorithms so words transfer permanently into your long-term memory.

  • Contextual examples in everyday English & Bangla
  • Bengali script (লিপি) + phonetic Banglish transliteration
  • Cultural idioms (বাগধারা) and proverbs with nuances

Grammar Practice

Demystify sentence structures without tedious rote memorization. Side-by-side comparative breakdowns show how English SVO maps to Bengali SOV intuitively.

  • Verb conjugations across past, present, and future tenses
  • Polite vs casual pronoun registers (আপনি / তুমি / তুই)
  • Interactive sentence tile reordering exercises

English Syntax (S-V-O)

"I eat rice"
Subject + Verb + Object

Bangla Syntax (S-O-V)

"আমি ভাত খাই"
Subject + Object + Verb

Pronunciation Training

Conquer tricky phonetic challenges with real-time feedback. Master unique Bengali retroflex letters (ট, ঠ, ড) and English diphthongs with audio speed control.

  • Syllable-by-syllable breakdowns & native voice recordings
  • Aspirated vs non-aspirated consonant discrimination
  • 0.75x slow-playback mode for pitch & stress mastery
